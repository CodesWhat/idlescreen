title: Camera mode

Camera is one of the savers in Scene. It renders your live camera as animated characters, with a Camera Color palette and a Mirror camera toggle. You choose the camera in Scene, and IdleScreen recovers automatically after permission or device changes.

## Turning it on

Camera access is requested only when you explicitly choose a camera effect, or from the optional third step of [setup](/getting-started#first-run). Scene's camera card tells you where you stand:

| State | What the card says | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not asked yet | Allow camera access | Click Allow camera access. macOS shows its prompt. |
| Denied | Camera access is off | Turn it on in Camera privacy settings. |
| Restricted | Camera access is restricted | A policy on this Mac blocks the camera. Ask whoever manages it. |
| Allowed but failing | The camera isn't working | Open Settings to check it. |

If setup isn't finished, the card asks you to finish setup first. The preview badge in Scene reads Camera Live, Camera Starting, Camera Unavailable or Camera Checking.

If a camera frame is unavailable or stale, the renderer falls back to a procedural source.

## Privacy

- Camera frames stay inside the local App Group mailbox. They are not sent over the network, written to logs or retained as test evidence.
- A separately signed camera agent is the only process allowed to open the camera.
- Capture starts when the first valid consumer asks for it and stops after the last one ends.
- Diagnostics never contain camera pixels.

See [Architecture](/architecture#camera-lifecycle) for the lease and mailbox design, and [Security and privacy](/security).

Granting and revoking permission, camera contention with another app and disconnecting the selected camera are on the [not verified on a device](/troubleshooting#not-verified-on-a-device) list.

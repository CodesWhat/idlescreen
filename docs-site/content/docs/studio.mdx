The companion window has four pages in its top navigation: Scene, Displays, Agents and Settings. Scene gives most of the window to the screen saver preview, with a visible scene library on the left and a compact control row along the bottom.

The window resizes freely. The preview keeps the selected display’s proportions, including portrait and ultrawide screens. Use the display picker above the preview to choose a screen. Browse the scene thumbnails and names in the library. Hide library gives the preview more room; Show library brings the choices back. More, or the inspector button, opens every adjustment on the right. IdleScreen remembers your inspector choices and selected display between launches.

## Controls

The compact row has speed or cell scale, palette, glyph size and contrast. Open More for the full inspector. The inspector has native controls for the current scene: speed, scale, glyph size and contrast, where they apply. Pattern-specific adjustments are under Pattern, and quality is under Rendering. The palette swatches select Cobalt, Ember, Phosphor, Ivory, Blueprint or Signal. Camera Color is also available for camera looks.

## Pixel Materials

Pixel Materials runs sand and water simulations. Scene state is deterministic: the same seed gives the same world, and New draws a new seed.

- **Material**: sand, water or mixed.
- **Terrain**: watershed, terraces or caverns.
- **Palette**: canyon, tidal or monochrome.
- **Watershed**: basin count and the shape of the basins.
- **Emitters**: count, position, width and rate.
- **Flow**: cell scale, gravity, lateral flow, equalization, water pressure, spill rate, drain rate, evaporation rate, obstacle density and persistence.

Pixel Materials uses the same display boundary as every other pattern, so terrain and world edges stay stable across view recreation and display changes. Edge behavior for several screens is set on the [Displays](/displays) page.

## Saved Looks

A Saved Look brings back an exact saver and its styling in one click. Open Your looks in the library:

- **Save look…** at the bottom of the inspector stores the current look and opens Your looks. You can keep up to 32.
- Click a look to apply it.
- Right-click a look for **Apply**, **Update from Current**, **Rename…** and **Delete…**.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- **Command-1 through Command-4**: Scene, Displays, Agents and Settings.
- **Option-Command-I**: show or hide the current inspector.
- **Shift-Command-L**: open or close the scene library.

Procedural scenes, including Topograph, offer Full Bleed or Horizon framing. Full Bleed keeps the pattern visible across the display and is the default for new configurations. Horizon adds a dim sky above the ground. In a panorama across stacked displays, one display may show mostly that sky. The framing menu above the preview changes this directly.
